description: "Germany has various copyright exceptions for illustration for teaching that fall under the scope of protection of Article 5(3)(a) of the InfoSoc Directive: one main exception for teaching in educational establishments (§60a), including specific conditions for computer programs (§69d(5)); one exception for the creation of media collections for teaching (§60b); and one exception for school broadcasts (§47). In addition, it has two copyright exceptions for scientific research: one main scientific research exception (§60c), and one specific text and data mining exception for scientific research that also allows the sharing of copies within the framework of a joint scientific research and for monitoring purposes (§60d). There are provisions stating that the exceptions to copyright apply to performances (§83), phonograms (§85(4)), broadcasts (§87(4)), press publications (§87i) and film fixations (§94)." 
beneficiaries:
- teachers and participants at the respective event; teachers and examiners at the same educational establishment; third persons insofar as this serves the presentation of lessons or lectures or the results of tuition or training or learning outcomes at the educational establishment (exception for teaching in educational establishments)
- producers of media collections for teaching (exception for media collections for teaching)
- schools, teacher training and further training institutions (exception for school broadcasts)
- a specifically delimited circle of persons for their personal scientific research; and individual third persons insofar as this serves the monitoring of the quality of scientific research (scientific research exception)
- research organizations (insofar as they pursue non-commercial purposes), libraries and museums, insofar as they are accessible to the public, and archives or institutions in the field of cinematic or audio heritage, and individual researchers (insofar as they pursue non-commercial purposes) (reproduction under TDM exception for scientific research)
- a specifically delimited circle of persons for their joint scientific research and individual third persons for the purpose of monitoring the quality of the scientific research (making available under TDM exception for scientific research)

purposes: 
- illustration in teaching in educational establishments, on a non-commercial basis (exception for teaching in educational establishments)
- for media collections which bring together a significant number of authors and which are suitable, intended and labelled accordingly for the exclusive purpose of non-commercial illustration in teaching in educational establishments (exception for media collections for teaching)
- teaching purposes (exception for school broadcasts)
- non-commercial scientific research (scientific research exception)
- to carry out text and data mining for scientific research purposes (reproduction under TDM exception for scientific research)
- for joint scientific research or for the purpose of monitoring the quality of the scientific research (making available under TDM exception for scientific research)

usage:
- reproduction, distribution, make available to the public and communication to the public of published works, performances, phonograms, broadcasts, film fixations, press publications (exception for teaching in educational establishments)
- reproduction, distribution, make available to the public, communication to the public, translation, adaptation, arrangement and other modifications of a computer program, as well as the reproduction of the results thereof (exception for teaching in educational establishments)
- reproduction, distribution, make available to the public (exception for media collections for teaching)
- make individual copies of works to be used as part of a school broadcast by transferring the works to video or audio recording mediums (exception for school broadcasts)
- reproduction, distribution, and making available to the public; it doesn’t apply however to the recording of the public recitation, performance or presentation of a work onto a video or audio recording medium and the subsequent making available to the public of that recording (scientific research exception)
- reproduction and make available to the public (TDM exception for scientific research)

subjectmatter:
- published works, performances, phonograms, broadcasts, press publications, film fixations (exception for teaching in educational establishments, exception for media collections for teaching)
- works, performances, phonograms, broadcasts, press publications, film fixations (exception for school broadcasts, scientific research exception, TDM exception for scientific research)

compensation: 
- no compensation is required (communication to the public (other than making the content available to the public), under exception for teaching in educational establishments, for the benefit of members of educational establishments and their families; exception for school broadcasts; reproductions under TDM exception for scientific research)
- compensation is required (all other uses under exception for teaching in educational establishments; exception for media collections for teaching; scientific research exception; making available to the public under TDM exception for scientific research)

attribution:
- the source must in all cases be clearly indicated; does not apply where the source is neither named on the work used or when the work used is communicated nor otherwise known to the person authorised to make the reproduction or distribution or the examination purposes necessitate dispensing with indicating the source (reproductions under exception for teaching in educational establishments)
- the source must be clearly indicated; does not apply where the source is neither named on the work used or when the work used is communicated nor otherwise known to the person authorised to make the reproduction or distribution (reproductions under exception for media collections for teaching; reproductions under exception for school broadcasts; reproductions under scientific research exception)
- the source including the author’s name, must in all cases be indicated, unless this is not possible (communication to the public and digital uses under exception for teaching in educational establishments; communication to the public under exception for media collections for teaching; communication to the public under scientific research exception; communication to the public under TDM exception for scientific research)
- the source is to be clearly indicated if and insofar as this is required by customary practice (communication to the public under exception for school broadcasts)

otherConditions: 
- quantity limitations: only allows the use of up to 15 percent of a published work; works can however be used in their entirety if they are illustrations, individual articles from the same professional or scientific journal, other small-scale works and out-of-commerce works; computer programs may also be used in their entirety (exception for teaching in educational establishments)
- licence availability: this exception does not apply to the following uses on the condition that licences for such uses are easily available and traceable, they meet the needs and specificities of educational establishments and permit such uses: reproduction of a work by means of recording onto video or audio recording mediums or communication to the public of a work whilst it is being publicly recited, performed or presented; reproduction, distribution and communication to the public of a work in schools which is exclusively suitable, intended and labelled for teaching in schools; reproduction of graphic recordings of musical works to the extent that such reproduction is not required for making content available to the public for the purposes covered by the exception (exception for teaching in educational establishments)
- quantity limitations: only allows the use of up to 10 percent of a published work, entire illustrations, individual articles from the same professional or scientific journal, other small-scale works and out-of-commerce works (exception for media collections for teaching)
- licence availability: this exception does not apply to the following uses on the condition that licences for such uses are easily available and traceable, they meet the needs and specificities of educational establishments and permit such uses: reproduction of a work by means of recording onto video or audio recording mediums or communication to the public of a work whilst it is being publicly recited, performed or presented (exception for media collections for teaching)
- time-limits: video or audio recording mediums used in school broadcast must be deleted at the latest at the end of the academic year following the transmission of the school broadcast, unless the author has been paid equitable remuneration (exception for school broadcasts)
- quantity limitations: only allows the use of up to 15 percent of a work; works can however be used in their entirety if they are illustrations, individual articles from the same professional or scientific journal, other small-scale works and out-of-commerce works; if the work is reproduced for personal scientific research, up to 75 percent of it can be used (scientific research exception)
- storage and retention: copies of works or other subject matter shall be stored with an appropriate level of security, and maybe retained for as long as they are needed for the purposes of the scientific research or the monitoring of the quality of the scientific findings (TDM exception for scientific research)
- time-limits: the act of making available to the public must be terminated as soon as the joint scientific research or the monitoring of the quality of the scientific research has been concluded (TDM exception for scientific research)

remarks: "The exception for teaching in educational establishments (§60a) allows the reproduction, distribution, making available to the public or communication to the public of up to 15 percent of a published work, entire illustrations, individual articles from the same professional or scientific journal, other small-scale works and out-of-commerce works, for the purpose of illustration in teaching in educational establishments, on a non-commercial basis. This exception is for the benefit of teachers and participants at the respective event; teachers and examiners at the same educational establishment; and third persons insofar as this serves the presentation of lessons or lectures or the results of tuition or training or learning outcomes at the educational establishment. This exception does not apply to the following uses, on the condition that licences for such uses are easily available and traceable, they meet the needs and specificities of educational establishments and permit such uses: reproduction of a work by means of recording onto video or audio recording mediums or communication to the public of a work whilst it is being publicly recited, performed or presented; reproduction, distribution and communication to the public of a work in schools which is exclusively suitable, intended and labelled for teaching in schools; reproduction of graphic recordings of musical works to the extent that such reproduction is not required for making content available to the public for the purposes covered by the exception. There's a provision stating that, where works are used in secure electronic environments for the purposes covered by the exception (except for third persons) in EU or EEA countries, such use is deemed only to have been effected in the Member State or Contracting Party in which the educational establishment is domiciled. The act of communication to the public (other than making the content available to the public) for the benefit of members of educational establishments and their families for the purposes covered by the provision, is not subject to compensation; all other uses are subject to compensation (§60h(2)1). <br />
The use of computer programs under the exception for teaching in educational establishments is subject to further conditions (§69d(5)): digital uses must take place under the responsibility of an educational establishment on its premises, at other locations or in a secure electronic environment; computer programs may be used in their entirety; computer programs may be subject to translation, adaptation, arrangement and other modifications of a computer program, as well as the reproduction of the results thereof. <br />
The exception for media collections for teaching (§60b) allows producers of media collections for teaching (collections which bring together a significant number of authors and which are suitable, intended and labelled accordingly for the exclusive purpose of non-commercial illustration in teaching in educational establishments) to reproduce, distribute or make available to the public up to 10 per cent of a published work, entire illustrations, individual articles from the same professional or scientific journal, other small-scale works and out-of-commerce works, for such collections. This exception does not apply to the following uses, on the condition that licences for such uses are easily available and traceable, they meet the needs and specificities of educational establishments and permit such uses: reproduction of a work by means of recording onto video or audio recording mediums or communication to the public of a work whilst it is being publicly recited, performed or presented. This exception does not apply to the uses covered by the exception for teaching in educational establishments and is subject to remuneration. <br />
The exception for school broadcasts (§47) allows schools and teacher training and further training institutions to make individual copies of works to be used as part of a school broadcast by transferring the works to video or audio recording mediums, which may only be used for teaching purposes and must be deleted at the latest at the end of the academic year following the transmission of the school broadcast, unless the author has been paid equitable remuneration. <br />
The scientific research exception (§60c) allows the reproduction, distribution, and making available to the public of up to 15 percent of a work, entire illustrations, individual articles from the same professional or scientific journal, other small-scale works and out-of-commerce works, for the purpose of non-commercial scientific research. If the work is reproduced for personal scientific research, up to 75 percent of it can be used. This exception is for the benefit of a specifically delimited circle of persons for their personal scientific research and for individual third persons insofar as this serves the monitoring of the quality of scientific research. This exception doesn’t apply to the recording of the public recitation, performance or presentation of a work onto a video or audio recording medium and the subsequent making available to the public of that recording. This exception is subject to compensation (§60h(1)). <br />
The exception for text and data mining for scientific research purposes (§60d) goes a step further than Article 3 of the DSM Directive, allowing not only reproductions of works to carry out text and data mining for scientific research purposes, but also the act of making available those reproductions to the public within the framework of a joint scientific research or for the purpose of monitoring the quality of the scientific research. Reproductions can be done by research organizations (insofar as they pursue non-commercial purposes), libraries and museums, insofar as they are accessible to the public, and archives or institutions in the field of cinematic or audio heritage, and individual researchers (insofar as they pursue non-commercial purposes). Those to whom the reproductions can be made available are: a specifically delimited circle of persons for their joint scientific research and individual third persons for the purpose of monitoring the quality of the scientific research. The act of making available to the public must be terminated as soon as the joint scientific research or the monitoring of the quality of the scientific research has been concluded. No compensation is required for reproductions; the act of making available to the public is subject to compensation (§60h(2)3). <br />
There are provisions stating that the exceptions to copyright apply to performances (§83), phonograms (§85(4)), broadcasts (§87(4)), press publications (§87i) and film fixations (§94)."
